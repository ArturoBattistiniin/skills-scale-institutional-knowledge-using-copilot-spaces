# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme manages projects through a consistent lifecycle designed to turn ideas into measurable outcomes and reliable delivery. Work begins with project initiation, where the team validates the business need, defines stakeholders, captures success metrics, and creates a lightweight project one-pager with milestones, risks, and resource needs. From there, planning translates approved work into a prioritized backlog, scope estimates, dependencies, and a clear Definition of Done so the team can execute with shared expectations.

Execution follows a defined rhythm using project boards, daily standups, weekly syncs, and milestone reviews to surface progress, blockers, and cross-team dependencies. The team tracks delivery through project status, quality checks, and regular demos, while risk and communication management ensure issues are escalated appropriately and stakeholders receive timely updates. When a feature is ready, the team follows release and deployment practices that include validation, rollback planning, smoke testing, and post-deploy verification. After each milestone or release, retrospectives capture lessons learned and convert them into clear action items for continuous improvement.

The process depends on clear ownership and collaboration across core roles. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, planning, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize what the team should build; Developers design and implement solutions; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input, approvals, and business context. Together, these roles support a transparent, customer-focused workflow that balances speed, quality, and accountability.
