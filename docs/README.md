# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme manages projects through a structured lifecycle that moves work from idea to delivery and continuous improvement. The process begins with project initiation, where teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, and produce a lightweight project one-pager with milestones, risks, and resource needs. Once approved, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog, clear acceptance criteria, dependencies, and a timeline with a defined Definition of Done.

Execution follows a regular rhythm of standups, weekly delivery syncs, and milestone demos to keep work visible and surface blockers quickly. The team uses a project board to track items from backlog through review and QA to completion, while risk and communication management ensures issues are escalated appropriately and stakeholders receive timely updates. Release and deployment practices focus on validation, smoke testing, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification to reduce risk. After each milestone or release, a retrospective captures lessons learned and turns them into action items that drive continuous improvement.

The process relies on clear ownership across core roles. Project managers coordinate schedules, risk, documentation, and communication; product managers define outcomes and prioritize what should be built; developers implement and test solutions; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide approval, business context, and strategic input. Together, these roles help keep work aligned, transparent, and focused on customer and business value.
